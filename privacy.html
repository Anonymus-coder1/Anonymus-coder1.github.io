Gki - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Gki (the "App") handles information when you connect your TikTok account.

2. Information we access

When you authorize the App through TikTok Login Kit, we may access basic profile information (such as your display name and avatar) and the permissions you explicitly grant (such as uploading and publishing videos).

3. How we use information

This information is used only to let you upload and publish your own videos to your own TikTok account. We do not use it for advertising or profiling.

4. Storage and sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with third parties. Access tokens are used only to perform the actions you request and are not shared.

5. Data retention

We keep access tokens only as long as needed to provide the service. You can revoke the App's access at any time from your TikTok account settings (Security and permissions), after which we can no longer access your account.

6. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information we handle, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. Children

The App is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

8. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this address.
